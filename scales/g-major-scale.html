📚 About G Major Scale

G Major contains only one sharp/black key (Gb), which makes it a very friendly scale for beginners. It has a warm, grounded sound and is highly popular in folk music, devotional songs, and traditional hymns.

🎼 Scale Intervals

Follows the standard Major scale step pattern starting on G, introducing Gb as the leading note.
