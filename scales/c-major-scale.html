📚 About C Major Scale

The C Major scale consists of seven notes: C, D, E, F, G, A, and B. Since it has no sharps or flats, it is the easiest scale to learn and play on the piano or virtual harmonium. Practicing C Major helps build finger strength, dexterity, and a clean mental model of interval spacing.

🎼 Scale Intervals

The step pattern for a Major scale is: Tone - Tone - Semitone - Tone - Tone - Tone - Semitone (W-W-H-W-W-W-H).
