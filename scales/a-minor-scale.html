📚 About A Minor Scale

A Natural Minor shares the exact same notes as C Major but starts and resolves on A. This shift in the root note completely changes the emotional color of the music, turning it from bright and cheerful to melancholic and contemplative (equivalent to Asavari Thaat notes).

🎼 Scale Intervals

The step pattern for a Natural Minor scale is: Tone - Semitone - Tone - Tone - Semitone - Tone - Tone (W-H-W-W-H-W-W).
