Our Story & Team

Democratizing Harmonium Education

The harmonium is the heartbeat of South Asian music — from classical gharanas to Sufi qawwali, from devotional bhajans to Bollywood compositions. Yet quality harmonium education has always required expensive teachers, physical books, or paid apps.

LearnHarmonium was built with a single mission: make harmonium learning completely free and accessible to everyone. Whether you're a curious beginner in Lahore, a diaspora musician in London, or a classical student in Mumbai — this platform is for you.

Ustad Ahmed Khan (Musical Advisor) is a classical vocalist and harmonium player with over 30 years of experience teaching Hindustani classical music, ensuring all notations are historically and theoretically precise.

Zainab Rahman (Lead Developer) is a software engineer specializing in web-based audio synthesis. She built the Web Audio API synthesis engine to replicate warm harmonium tones in the browser.
