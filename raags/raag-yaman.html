📚 About Raag Yaman

Raag Yaman belongs to the Kalyan Thaat. The singing time is Evening (9 PM – 12 AM), which evokes a Romantic, Devotional, Serene mood. The Vadi note is Ga and Samvadi note is Ni. Use the interactive harmonium keyboard above to practice this raag. Click 'Play Aaroh' or 'Play Avaroh' to hear and watch the correct note sequence.

🎼 Notes in Raag Yaman

The Raag Yaman uses the following sargam notes. Golden keys on the harmonium above are highlighted for you to follow along:

Sa (C) Re (D) Ga (E) Ma# (Gb) Pa (G) Dha (A) Ni (B)
